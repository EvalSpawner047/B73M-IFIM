OMR 001-018;
AIRPLANES WITH INOP NO SMOKING SWITCH
Chimes Does Not Turn On - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
The chime in the passenger cabin does not sound when one or all of these conditions occur:
(a)
Attendant call button on the passenger service unit (PSU) is pushed
(b)
Lavatory call button is pushed
(c)
NO SMOKING switch is set to the ON position
(d)
FASTEN BELTS switch is set to the ON position.
B.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
For the passenger address amplifier, M63, do the "Installation Test" part of this task:
  • Passenger Address (PA) Amplifier Installation, AMM TASK 23-31-01-400-801.
(a)
If the system does not operate as specified in the AMM task, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (No Chime From PA Amplifier) below.
(b)
If the system operates as specified in the AMM task, then continue.
(2)
Do the "Do a test of the chime operation" part of this task:
  • Passenger Address System - Operational Test, AMM TASK 23-31-00-740-801.
(a)
If you did not hear a high chime on the PA speakers when you pushed the attendant call button on a Passenger Service Unit (PSU), then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (No Chime From PSU) below.
(b)
If you did not hear a high chime on the PA speakers when you pushed the attendant call button on the lavatory PSU, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (No Chime From Lavatory PSU) below.
(c)
If you did not hear a high/low chime on the PA speakers when you pushed the ATT button on the attendant handset, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (No Chime From Attendant Handset) below.
(d)
If you did not hear a high/low chime on the PA speakers when you pushed the ATTEND button on the forward overhead panel, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (No Chime From Forward Overhead Panel) below.
(e)
If you did not hear a low chime on the PA speakers when you set the FASTEN BELTS switch to the ON position, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (No Chime From FASTEN BELTS Switch) below.
(f)
If the system operated as specified in the AMM task, then there was an intermittent fault.

Fault Isolation Procedure (No Chime From Forward Overhead Panel)
(1)
Do this check of the continuity through the FASTEN BELTS switch (flight deck) (WDM 33-25-11):
(a)
Disconnect connector D1622 from the fasten-seat-belts relay, R26.
(b)
For each switch position below, set the switch to the specified position and do a check for continuity between the specified pins on the fasten-seat-belts relay:
1)
Switch position ON, make sure there is continuity between pin X2 and pin Ground.
(c)
If there was a problem with the continuity, then do these steps:
1)
Replace the FASTEN BELTS switch (flight deck), S46.
2)
Re-connect connector D1622 to the fasten-seat-belts relay, R26.
3)
Push the ATTEND button on the forward overhead panel.
4)
If you hear a high/low chime on the PA speakers in the passenger cabin, then you corrected the fault.
(d)
If there was not a problem with the continuity, then continue.
(2)
Do this check of the voltage at the fasten-seat-belts relay:
(a)
Do a check for voltage between pin X1 of connector D1622 and ground (WDM 33-25-11). The correct voltage is 28 VDC.
(b)
If the correct voltage is not present, then do this check of the wiring:
1)
Open the PASSENGER CABIN SIGNS PASS SIGN CONT circuit breaker, C442 [P18-3, D11].
2)
Do a wiring check between this pin of connector D1622 at the fasten-seat-belts relay and this terminal of circuit breaker C442 (WDM 33-25-11):
D1622C442
pin X1--------------------term =L
3)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
a)
Repair the wiring.
b)
Re-connect connector D1622 to the fasten-seat-belts relay, R26.
c)
Close the PASSENGER CABIN SIGNS PASS SIGN CONT circuit breaker, C442 [P18-3, D11].
d)
Push the ATTEND button on the forward overhead panel.
e)
If you hear a high/low chime on the PA speakers in the passenger cabin, then you corrected the fault.
4)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
a)
Re-connect connector D1622 to the fasten-seat-belts relay, R26.
b)
Close the PASSENGER CABIN SIGNS PASS SIGN CONT circuit breaker, C442 [P18-3, D11].
c)
Replace the PASSENGER CABIN SIGNS PASS SIGN CONT circuit breaker, C442.
d)
Push the ATTEND button on the forward overhead panel.
e)
If you hear a high/low chime on the PA speakers in the passenger cabin, then you corrected the fault.
(c)
If the correct voltage is present, then continue.

Replace the fasten-seat-belts relay, R26.
(a)
