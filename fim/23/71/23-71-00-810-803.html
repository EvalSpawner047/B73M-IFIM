Voice Recorder STATUS Light or TEST Light Does Not Come On - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
When you push the TEST button on the CVR Control Panel for approximately one half second, the STATUS or TEST light does not come on momentarily.
(2)
When you push the TEST button on the CVR Control Panel for approximately 5 seconds, the STATUS or TEST light does not come on.
B.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
Do this task:
  • Voice Recorder System - Operational Test, AMM TASK 23-71-00-710-801.
(a)
If the system does not operate as specified in the AMM task, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure below.
(b)
If the system operates as specified in the AMM task, then there was an intermittent fault.
C.
Fault Isolation Procedure
OMR 001-018
(1)
Do this check of the wiring:
(a)
Disconnect connector D14169 from the voice recorder module, M383.
(b)
Remove the communication management unit, M2127. This is the task:
  • ACARS Communications Management Unit (CMU) Removal, AMM TASK 23-27-33-020-801.
(c)
Do a wiring check between these pins of connector D14169 at the voice recorder module and these pins of connector D10727B at the E4-1 RACK (WDM 23-71-01, 23-27-35):
D14169D10727B
pin 34--------------------pin C15
pin 33--------------------pin D15
(d)
Do a wiring check to make sure that pin 56 of D14169 has continuity to ground.
(e)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the wiring.
2)
Re-connect connector D14169 to the voice recorder module, M383.
3)
Re-install the communication management unit, M2127. This is the task:
  • ACARS Communications Management Unit (CMU) Installation, AMM TASK 23-27-33-420-801.
4)
Do the AMM test that you did at the start of this task.
5)
If the system operates as specified in the AMM task, then you corrected the fault.
(f)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then continue.
(g)
Re-connect connector D14169 to the voice recorder module, M383.
(h)
Re-install the communication management unit, M2127. This is the task:
  • ACARS Communications Management Unit (CMU) Installation, AMM TASK 23-27-33-420-801.
(2)
Do this check of the voltage at the cockpit voice recorder dispatch relay:
(a)
Disconnect connector D13187 from the cockpit voice recorder dispatch relay, R1069.
(b)
Do a check for voltage between pin 7 of connector D13187 and ground (WDM 23-71-01). The correct voltage is 28 VDC.
(c)
If the correct voltage is not present, then do this check of the wiring:
1)
Open the VOICE RCDR RELAY circuit breaker, C1537.
2)
Do a wiring check between this pin of connector D13187 at the cockpit voice recorder dispatch relay and this terminal of circuit breaker C1537 (WDM 23-71-01):
D13187C1537
pin 7--------------------term =L
3)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
a)
Repair the wiring.
b)
Re-connect connector D13187 to the cockpit voice recorder dispatch relay, R1069.
c)
Close the VOICE RCDR RELAY circuit breaker, C1537.
d)
Do the AMM test that you did at the start of this task.
e)
If the system operates as specified in the AMM task, then you corrected the fault.
4)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
a)
Re-connect connector D13187 to the cockpit voice recorder dispatch relay, R1069.
b)
Replace the VOICE RCDR RELAY circuit breaker, C1537.
c)
Do the AMM test that you did at the start of this task.
d)
If the system operates as specified in the AMM task, then you corrected the fault.
(d)
If the correct voltage is present, then continue.
(e)
Re-connect connector D13187 to the cockpit voice recorder dispatch relay, R1069.
