Fuel Tank Unwanted Fuel Migration From Center Tank To Main Tank (To Tank 1) - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
Fuel moved from the Center Tank to the No. 1 Tank without a commanded fuel transfer.
(2)
Usually, fuel transfer into the No. 1 Tank is observed during flight, while the boost pumps operate. Fuel transfer from the Center Tank to the No. 1 tank is usually caused by failure of the Fuel Scavenge Float Valve to close completely, or by leakage in the Left Boost Pump Bypass Valve when it is pressurized by the Center Tank Boost Pump (Override Pump). Unwanted particles (debris) can cause the Boost Pump Bypass Valve to leak fuel into No. 1 Tank. Even small particles can cause a valve not to seal correctly. This can cause a large quantity of fuel to transfer. Unwanted fuel transfer can also be caused by leakage in the Boost Pump Discharge Check Valves, other parts of the Engine Fuel-Feed Manifold, the Fueling Manifold or the tank wall.
(3)
The Fuel Scavenge System has a minimum transfer rate of 220 pounds/hour (100 kilograms/hour). Usually, fuel scavenge transfer rates are between approximately 220 pounds/hour (100 kilograms/hour) and 450 pounds/hour (200 kilograms/hour).
B.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
Make sure that the main fuel tank 1 contains more than 6500 pounds (3000 kilograms) of fuel.
(2)
Make sure that the center fuel tank contains more than 1000 pounds (460 kilograms) of fuel.
(3)
Make sure that the crossfeed valve is closed.
(4)
Do these steps to do a check for leakage through the fuel scavenge float operated shutoff valve:
(a)
Set the FUEL PUMPS 1 FWD switch (flight deck) to ON.
(b)
Let the FWD pump for the main fuel tank 1 operate for 30 minutes.
(c)
While the pump operates, monitor the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 and the center fuel tank.
(d)
Set the FUEL PUMPS 1 FWD switch (flight deck) to OFF.
(e)
If the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 increases or the fuel quantity in the center fuel tank decreases, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Fuel Scavenge System) below.
(f)
If the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 does not increase and the fuel quantity in the center fuel tank does not decrease, then continue.
(5)
Do these steps to do a check for leakage through the fuel boost pump bypass valve or the engine fuel feed system:
(a)
Set the FUEL PUMPS CTR L switch (flight deck) to ON.
(b)
Let the FWD pump for the center fuel tank operate for 30 minutes.
(c)
While the pump operates, monitor the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 and the center fuel tank.
(d)
Set the FUEL PUMPS CTR L switch (flight deck) to OFF.
(e)
If the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 increases or the fuel quantity in the center fuel tank decreases, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Engine Fuel Feed System) below.
(f)
If the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 does not increase and the fuel quantity in the center fuel tank does not decrease, then continue.
(6)
If you do not find signs of unwanted fuel transfer on subsequent flights, then there was an intermittent fault.
C.
Fault Isolation Procedure (Fuel Scavenge System)
(1)
Do these steps to look for fuel leakage in the fuel scavenge system:
 OBEY THE PRECAUTIONS FOR PURGING AND FUEL TANK ENTRY. IF YOU DO NOT OBEY THE PRECAUTIONS, INJURIES TO PERSONNEL AND DAMAGE TO EQUIPMENT CAN OCCUR.
(a)
Prepare to go into the main fuel tank 1. This is the task:
  • Purging and Fuel Tank Entry, AMM TASK 28-11-00-910-802.
(b)
Examine the fuel scavenge float operated shutoff valve to make sure that it operates correctly. Look for unwanted material that could keep the valve open.
(c)
If you find problems with the fuel scavenge float operated shutoff valve, then do these steps:
1)
Replace the fuel scavenge float operated shutoff valve. These are the tasks:
  • Fuel Scavenge Float Operated Shutoff Valve Removal, AMM TASK 28-22-16-000-801,
  • Fuel Scavenge Float Operated Shutoff Valve Installation, AMM TASK 28-22-16-400-801.
2)
Make sure that the main fuel tank 1 contains more than 6500 pounds (3000 kilograms) of fuel.
3)
Make sure that the center fuel tank contains more than 1000 pounds (460 kilograms) of fuel.
4)
Make sure that the crossfeed valve is closed.
5)
Do these steps to do a check for leakage through the fuel scavenge float operated shutoff valve:
a)
Set the FUEL PUMPS 1 FWD switch (flight deck) to ON.
b)
Let the FWD pump for the main fuel tank 1 operate for 30 minutes.
c)
While the pump operates, monitor the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 and the center fuel tank.
d)
Set the FUEL PUMPS 1 FWD switch (flight deck) to OFF.
e)
If the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 does not increase and the fuel quantity in the center fuel tank does not decrease, then you corrected the fault.
(d)
If you do not find problems with the fuel scavenge float operated shutoff valve, then do these steps:
1)
Do a check for leakage at the fuel scavenge couplings of the fuel scavenge tubing in the main fuel tank 1.
2)
If necessary, replace the applicable couplings in the fuel scavenge system. These are the tasks:
  • Fuel Line, Fitting and Coupling Removal, AMM TASK 28-22-15-000-801,
  • Fuel Line, Fitting and Coupling Installation, AMM TASK 28-22-15-400-801.
3)
Make sure that the main fuel tank 1 contains more than 6500 pounds (3000 kilograms) of fuel.
4)
Make sure that the center fuel tank contains more than 1000 pounds (460 kilograms) of fuel.
5)
Make sure that the crossfeed valve is closed.
6)
Do these steps to do a check for leakage through the fuel scavenge float operated shutoff valve:
a)
Set the FUEL PUMPS 1 FWD switch (flight deck) to ON.
b)
Let the FWD pump for the main fuel tank 1 operate for 30 minutes.
c)
While the pump operates, monitor the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 and the center fuel tank.
d)
Set the FUEL PUMPS 1 FWD switch (flight deck) to OFF.
e)
If the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 does not increase and the fuel quantity in the center fuel tank does not decrease, then you corrected the fault.
f)
If the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 increases or the fuel quantity in the center fuel tank decreases, then continue.
(2)
Do a check for leakage of the tubing of the fuel scavenge system in the main fuel tank 1.
(a)
If you find a problem with the tubing, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the problem that you found.
2)
Make sure that the main fuel tank 1 contains more than 6500 pounds (3000 kilograms) of fuel.
3)
Make sure that the center fuel tank contains more than 1000 pounds (460 kilograms) of fuel.
4)
Make sure that the crossfeed valve is closed.
5)
Do these steps to do a check for leakage through the fuel scavenge float operated shutoff valve:
a)
Set the FUEL PUMPS 1 FWD switch (flight deck) to ON.
b)
Let the FWD pump for the main fuel tank 1 operate for 30 minutes.
c)
While the pump operates, monitor the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 and the center fuel tank.
d)
Set the FUEL PUMPS 1 FWD switch (flight deck) to OFF.
e)
If the fuel quantity in the main fuel tank 1 does not increase and the fuel quantity in the center fuel tank does not decrease, then you corrected the fault.
