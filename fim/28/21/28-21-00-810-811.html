Refuel Panel Quantity Indicator Display Incorrect With Switch At TEST GAGES (Tank 2) - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
You push the FUELING INDICATION TEST SWITCH, S160 on the P15 Fueling Control Panel to the TEST GAGES position and one or more or the indicators does not enter the test mode as expected (all segments alternating on then off at two second intervals). If one of the indicators shows Ind FAIL, this shows an internal fault in the applicable indicator. The indicators can also be blank or show one or more missing segments. If the SWITCH is held for more than twenty (20) seconds, the test stops automatically and the indicators go back to indicating mode.
B.
Fault Isolation Procedure
(1)
Use the CDU to do the FQIS GROUND TEST. This is the task:
  • Fuel Quantity Indicating System - Operational Test, AMM TASK 28-41-00-710-801.
(a)
When the test completes, look in OMF Existing Faults, 28 FUEL QUANTITY INDICATION SYSTEM for related maintenance messages.
(b)
If you find related maintenance messages, then do these steps:
1)
Do the fault isolation task for each related maintenance message.
2)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(c)
If you do not find related maintenance messages, then continue.
(2)
Do this check of the voltage at the tank 2 fueling indicator:
(a)
Disconnect connector D11320 from the tank 2 fueling indicator, N194.
(b)
Do a check for 28 VDC between pins 24 and 23 of connector D11320 (WDM 28-44-11).
(c)
If 28 VDC is present, then do these steps:
1)
Replace the tank 2 fueling indicator, N194. These are the tasks:
  • Refuel Quantity Indicator Removal, AMM TASK 28-41-61-000-801,
  • Refuel Quantity Indicator Installation, AMM TASK 28-41-61-400-801.
2)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(d)
If 28 VDC is not present, then continue.
(3)
Do this check of the wiring:
(a)
Disconnect connector D944 from the ground refueling power control relay, R11.
(b)
Do a wiring check between this pin of connector D11320 at the tank 2 fueling indicator and this pin of connector D944 at the ground refueling power control relay (WDM 28-44-11):
D11320D944
pin 24--------------------pin D2
(c)
Do a wiring check to make sure that pin 23 of D11320 has continuity to ground.
(d)
Do a wiring check to make sure that pin 19 of D11320 has continuity to ground.
(e)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the wiring.
2)
Re-connect connector D11320 to the tank 2 fueling indicator, N194.
3)
Re-connect connector D944 to the ground refueling power control relay, R11.
4)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(f)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then continue.
(g)
Re-connect connector D944 to the ground refueling power control relay, R11.
(4)
Do this check of the wiring:
(a)
Disconnect the wires from the refuel panel TEST GAUGES/FUEL DOOR SWITCH BYPASS switch, S160.
(b)
Do a wiring check between these wire terminals at the refuel panel TEST GAUGES/FUEL DOOR SWITCH BYPASS switch and this pin of connector D11320 at the tank 2 fueling indicator (WDM 28-44-11):
S160D11320
term 5--------------------pin 2
(c)
Do a wiring check to make sure that wire terminal 6 of S160 has continuity to ground.
(d)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the wiring.
2)
Re-connect the wires to the refuel panel TEST GAUGES/FUEL DOOR SWITCH BYPASS switch, S160.
3)
Re-connect connector D11320 to the tank 2 fueling indicator, N194.
4)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(e)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then continue.
(f)
Re-connect connector D11320 to the tank 2 fueling indicator, N194.
(5)
Do this check of the continuity through the refuel panel TEST GAUGES/FUEL DOOR SWITCH BYPASS switch (WDM 28-44-11):
(a)
For each switch position below, set the switch to the specified position and do a check for continuity between the specified pins on the switch:
1)
Switch position TEST GAUGES, make sure there is continuity between wire terminal 5 and wire terminal 6.
(b)
If there was a problem with the continuity, then do these steps:
1)
Replace the refuel panel TEST GAUGES/FUEL DOOR SWITCH BYPASS switch, S160.
2)
Re-connect the wires to the refuel panel TEST GAUGES/FUEL DOOR SWITCH BYPASS switch, S160.
3)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
