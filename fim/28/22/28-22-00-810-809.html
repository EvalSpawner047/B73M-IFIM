Fuel Pumps LOW PRESSURE Light Is On (CTR R) - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
The LOW PRESSURE light for the right center tank boost pump (override pump) is on. This means that the right center tank boost pump is not pressurizing the fuel feed manifold.
(a)
If either one or both LOW PRESSURE lights for the center tank come on for at least 10 seconds, then the FUEL light on the pilots master caution panel will come on.
(2)
This fault condition is expected to occur during flight when the center tank becomes empty. The auto shutoff system will turn off the center boost pumps after a 15 second time delay if the switches for the center boost pumps are in the ON position. The pilot usually turns off the center tank boost pumps at this time. The low pressure lights for the center tank boost pumps should go off when the switches for the center tank boost pumps are in the OFF position.
(3)
This fault condition is expected to occur during flight when the center tank becomes empty. The pilot usually turns off the center tank boost pumps at this time. If the center tank boost pump continues to operate when it has been commanded off, a series connected pump relay, R963, will provide the means of inhibiting the fuel pump through redundancy in turning off the pump.
(4)
This fault condition can also occur because the center boost pump has lost its prime. If the center tank is filled to 14000 lb (6350 kg), as described in the initial evaluation, the boost pump will become primed. You can also use one of the alternative boost pump priming procedures.
B.
Initial Evaluation
 DO NOT OPERATE A FUEL PUMP IF THE LOW PRESSURE LIGHT COMES ON AND STAYS ON. THIS CONDITION CAN CAUSE THE IGNITION OF THE FUEL FUMES IN THE FUEL TANK. A FIRE OR AN EXPLOSION CAN CAUSE INJURIES TO PERSONNEL AND DAMAGE TO EQUIPMENT.
(1)
While you do this task, do not operate a fuel pump if its LOW PRESSURE light stays on.
(a)
ALI - For important information on Airworthiness Limitation Instructions, refer to this task:
  • Airworthiness Limitation Precautions, AMM TASK 28-00-00-910-801.
(b)
This is applicable to Airworthiness Limitation 28-AWL-19 and 28-AWL-23.
 DO NOT CLOSE A CIRCUIT BREAKER FOR A FUEL PUMP THAT OPENED (TRIPPED) UNTIL YOU CORRECT THE PROBLEM. THIS CONDITION CAN CAUSE A FIRE OR EXPLOSION.
(2)
Look at the state of the FUEL BOOST PUMP CTR TANK RIGHT circuit breaker, C846. If the breaker is open, then before you continue you must do the fault isolation task for the applicable "Fuel tank pump circuit breaker open" observed fault.
(a)
CDCCL - For important information on Critical Design Configuration Control Limitations, refer to this task:
  • Airworthiness Limitation Precautions, AMM TASK 28-00-00-910-801.
(b)
This is applicable to Airworthiness Limitation 28-AWL-18.
 DO NOT CLOSE A GFI RELAY FOR A FUEL PUMP THAT OPENED (TRIPPED) UNTIL YOU CORRECT THE PROBLEM. THIS CONDITION CAN CAUSE A FIRE OR EXPLOSION.
(3)
Look at the state of the center right boost pump ground fault interrupter relay, R55. If its RESET button is out, then before you continue you must do the fault isolation task for the applicable "Fuel tank pump ground fault interrupter relay open" observed fault.
NOTE:  When the relay opens, the RESET button at the top of the relay moves out and shows a white band. The relay opens when there is a ground fault or when you push the TEST button on the relay.
(a)
CDCCL - For important information on Critical Design Configuration Control Limitations, refer to this task:
  • Airworthiness Limitation Precautions, AMM TASK 28-00-00-910-801.
(b)
This is applicable to Airworthiness Limitation 28-AWL-18.
(4)
Make sure that the fuel tank has the specified minimum quantity of fuel. This makes sure that the boost pump is correctly primed.
(a)
For the center tank, the minimum quantity is 6350 kg (14,000 lb).
(b)
For main tank 1 or main tank 2, the minimum quantity is 227 kg (500 lb).
(c)
If it is not possible to add fuel to the tank, then do this task:
  • Fuel Boost Pump and Override Pump Priming, AMM TASK 28-22-41-420-801.
(5)
Two people are necessary to do the next steps. One person will operate the switch in the flight deck, and the other person will listen to the boost pump in the wheel well.
(a)
Set the FUEL PUMPS CTR R switch (flight deck) to ON.
(b)
If the fuel pumps LOW PRESSURE CTR R light (flight deck), P5-2L15 comes ON, and then goes OFF, after a maximum of 90 seconds, then there was an intermittent fault.
(c)
Listen to the pump and touch the pump. If the pump operates, you will hear it and feel it.
(d)
Set the FUEL PUMPS CTR R switch to OFF.
(6)
If the pump operated, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Pump Operates) below.
(7)
If the pump did not operate, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Pump Does Not Operate) below.
C.
Fault Isolation Procedure (Pump Operates)
(1)
This observed fault can show when there is not a failure in the system.
(a)
Additional information related to this observed fault is detailed in the Fault Description section.
(2)
Do this task:
  • Fuel Boost Pump Output Pressure - Functional Test, AMM TASK 28-22-00-720-804.
(a)
Set the FUEL PUMPS CTR R switch to ON.
(b)
If the fuel pumps LOW PRESSURE CTR R light (flight deck) is off, then you corrected the fault.
1)
Set the FUEL PUMPS CTR R switch to OFF.
(c)
If the fuel pumps LOW PRESSURE CTR R light is on, then continue.
(3)
Replace the center tank right boost pump low pressure switch, S155. These are the tasks:
  • Fuel Boost Pump Fuel Pressure Switch Removal, AMM TASK 28-42-11-000-801,
  • Fuel Boost Pump Fuel Pressure Switch Installation, AMM TASK 28-42-11-420-801.
(a)
Set the FUEL PUMPS CTR R switch to ON.
(b)
If the fuel pumps LOW PRESSURE CTR R light (flight deck) is off, then you corrected the fault.
1)
Set the FUEL PUMPS CTR R switch to OFF.
(c)
If the fuel pumps LOW PRESSURE CTR R light is on, then continue.
(4)
Do this check of the wiring:
(a)
Disconnect connector D628 from the fuel system module, P5-2.
(b)
Disconnect connector D878 from the center tank right boost pump low pressure switch, S155.
(c)
Do a wiring check between this pin of connector D628 at the fuel system module and this pin of connector D878 at the center tank right boost pump low pressure switch (WDM 28-43-11):
D628D878

3)
Re-connect connector D878 to the center tank right boost pump low pressure switch, S155.

Set the FUEL PUMPS CTR R switch to ON.
5)
If the fuel pumps LOW PRESSURE CTR R light (flight deck) is off, then you corrected the fault.

Re-connect connector D878 to the center tank right boost pump low pressure switch, S155.

  • Fuel System Module, P5-2 Removal, AMM TASK 28-22-43-020-801,
