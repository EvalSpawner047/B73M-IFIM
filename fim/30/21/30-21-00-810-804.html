OMR 001 PRE SB 737-30-1076
ENG ANTI-ICE COWL VALVE Light Is On At More Than 28000 Feet (ENG 2 COWL VALVE Light) - Fault Isolation
A.
Fault Isolation Procedure
(1)
Look in OMF Existing Faults, 36 Pneumatics for related maintenance messages.
(a)
If you find any related maintenance messages, then do these steps:
1)
Do the fault isolation task for each related maintenance message.
2)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(b)
If you do not find related maintenance messages, then continue.
(2)
Do this check of the wiring:
(a)
Remove the right air data/inertial reference unit, M1752. This is the task:
  • Air Data Inertial Reference Unit Removal, AMM TASK 34-21-01-000-801.
(b)
Disconnect connector D648 from the engine and wing anti-ice panel, P5-11.
(c)
Do a wiring check between this pin of connector D3693A at the E5-2 rack and this pin of connector D648 at the engine and wing anti-ice panel (WDM 34-21-21):
D3693AD648
pin F2--------------------pin 26
(d)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the wiring.
2)
Re-install the right air data/inertial reference unit, M1752. This is the task:
  • Air Data Inertial Reference Unit Installation, AMM TASK 34-21-01-400-801.
3)
Re-connect connector D648 to the engine and wing anti-ice panel, P5-11.
4)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(e)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then continue.
(f)
Re-install the right air data/inertial reference unit, M1752. This is the task:
  • Air Data Inertial Reference Unit Installation, AMM TASK 34-21-01-400-801.
(3)
Do this check of the wiring:
(a)
Open the ANTI-ICE & RAIN ENG 2 COWL AI VALVE circuit breaker, C1002 [P18-3, B7].
(b)
Do a wiring check between this terminal of circuit breaker C1002 and this pin of connector D648 at the engine and wing anti-ice panel (WDM 30-21-32):
D40538PD648
term 6--------------------pin 27
(c)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the wiring.
2)
Close the ANTI-ICE & RAIN ENG 2 COWL AI VALVE circuit breaker, C1002 [P18-3, B7].
3)
Re-connect connector D648 to the engine and wing anti-ice panel, P5-11.
4)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(d)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then continue.
(e)
Close the ANTI-ICE & RAIN ENG 2 COWL AI VALVE circuit breaker, C1002 [P18-3, B7].
(4)
Do this check of the wiring:
(a)
Disconnect connector DP302 from the cowl thermal anti-ice valve (engine 2), V193.
(b)
Do a wiring check between this pin of connector D648 at the engine and wing anti-ice panel and this pin of connector DP302 at the cowl thermal anti-ice valve (engine 2) (WDM 30-21-32):
D648DP302
pin 23--------------------pin 1
(c)
Do a wiring check to make sure that pin 2 of DP302 has continuity to ground.
(d)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the wiring.
2)
Re-connect connector D648 to the engine and wing anti-ice panel, P5-11.
3)
Re-connect connector DP302 to the cowl thermal anti-ice valve (engine 2), V193.
4)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(e)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then continue.
(f)
Re-connect connector DP302 to the cowl thermal anti-ice valve (engine 2), V193.
(5)
Do this check of the wiring:
(a)
Disconnect connector DP306 from the engine anti-ice minimum pressure switch, S1198.
(b)
Do a wiring check between this pin of connector D648 at the engine and wing anti-ice panel and this pin of connector DP306 at the engine anti-ice minimum pressure switch (WDM 30-21-32):
D648DP306
pin 31--------------------pin 1
(c)
Do a wiring check to make sure that pin 2 of DP306 has continuity to ground.
(d)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the wiring.
2)
Re-connect connector D648 to the engine and wing anti-ice panel, P5-11.
3)
Re-connect connector DP306 to the engine anti-ice minimum pressure switch, S1198.
4)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(e)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then continue.
(f)
Re-connect connector D648 to the engine and wing anti-ice panel, P5-11.
(g)
Re-connect connector DP306 to the engine anti-ice minimum pressure switch, S1198.
OMR 001 PRE SB 737-30-1075 AND PRE SB 737-30-1076
(6)
Do this check of the wiring:
(a)
Disconnect connector D648 from the engine and wing anti-ice panel, P5-11.
(b)
Disconnect connector DP305 from the engine anti-ice under pressure switch, S1199.
(c)
Do a wiring check between this pin of connector D648 at the engine and wing anti-ice panel and this pin of connector DP305 at the engine anti-ice under pressure switch (WDM 30-21-32):
D648DP305
pin 28--------------------pin 1
(d)
Do a wiring check to make sure that pin 2 of DP305 has continuity to ground.
(e)
If you find a problem with the wiring, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the wiring.
2)
Re-connect connector D648 to the engine and wing anti-ice panel, P5-11.
3)
Re-connect connector DP305 to the engine anti-ice under pressure switch, S1199.
4)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(f)
If you do not find a problem with the wiring, then continue.
(g)
Re-connect connector DP305 to the engine anti-ice under pressure switch, S1199.
OMR 001 PRE SB 737-30-1076
(7)
Replace the engine and wing anti-ice panel, P5-11. These are the tasks:
  • Engine and Wing Anti-Ice Panel Removal, AMM TASK 30-11-41-000-802,
  • Engine and Wing Anti-Ice Panel Installation, AMM TASK 30-11-41-400-802.
(a)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(b)
If the observed fault symptom stays, then continue.
(8)
Replace the cowl thermal anti-ice valve (engine 2), V193. These are the tasks:
  • Engine Anti-Ice Valve Removal, AMM TASK 30-21-11-000-801,
  • Engine Anti-Ice Valve Installation, AMM TASK 30-21-11-400-801.
(a)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(b)
If the observed fault symptom stays, then continue.
(9)
Replace the engine anti-ice minimum pressure switch, S1198. These are the tasks:
  • Engine Anti-Ice Pressure Switch Removal, AMM TASK 30-21-21-000-801,
  • Engine Anti-Ice Pressure Switch Installation, AMM TASK 30-21-21-400-801.
(a)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(b)
If the observed fault symptom stays, then continue.

(10)
Replace the engine anti-ice under pressure switch, S1199. These are the tasks:
  • Engine Anti-Ice Pressure Switch Removal, AMM TASK 30-21-21-000-801,
  • Engine Anti-Ice Pressure Switch Installation, AMM TASK 30-21-21-400-801.
(a)
If the observed fault symptom is gone, then you corrected the fault.
(b)
If the observed fault symptom stays, then continue.

  • Air Data Inertial Reference Unit Removal, AMM TASK 34-21-01-000-801,
  • Air Data Inertial Reference Unit Installation, AMM TASK 34-21-01-400-801.
