Tiller Steering Does Not Operate - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
Steering inputs are from the steering tiller or the rudder pedals. When you move the steering tiller full travel the nose wheels turn a maximum of 78 degrees in the left or right direction. Steering inputs from the tiller and rudder pedals go to the metering valve through a cable loop.
(2)
The nose wheel steering (NWS) system uses the hydraulic pressure for landing gear extension to turn the nose wheels.
(3)
The steering inputs go through the control cables NWSA (Nose Wheel Steering Control Cable A) and NWSB (Nose Wheel Steering Control Cable B) to the steering metering valve on the nose gear. The cables move the summing mechanism to provide in input to the metering valve. This input sends hydraulic pressure through the swivel valves to the steering actuators.
(4)
The two steering actuators get hydraulic pressure on the extend side, the retract side, or both sides. This moves the nose wheels, through the torsion links, up to 78 degrees to the left or right.
(5)
When the nose wheels get to the commanded position, the summing mechanism moves the metering valve back to neutral. This stops hydraulic pressure to the steering actuators and stops movement of the nose wheels.
B.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
Do this check of the nose wheel steering system with the nose landing gear steering control wheel (tiller).
(a)
Do the "Prepare to Test the Steering System of the Nose Wheel" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
(b)
Remove the towing lever lock pin and let the towing lever move to the NORMAL POSITION.
(c)
Do the "Test of the Normal Steering Response" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
(2)
If the nose wheel steering system does not operate correctly, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure below.
(3)
If the nose wheel steering system operates correctly, then there was an intermittent fault.
(a)
Do the "Put the Airplane Back to its Usual Condition" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
C.
Fault Isolation Procedure
(1)
Do a detailed visual inspection of the nose gear steering system cable loop. This is the task:
  • Nose Gear Steering System Cables Inspection, AMM TASK 32-51-31-220-801.
(a)
Look for friction in the pulleys and bearings, cable misalignment, incorrect routing of cables, broken or frayed cables, pulleys and cables contacting to the local structure.
(b)
If you find a problem during the inspection, then do these steps:
1)
If the nose gear steering system cable loop is broken or frayed, then do these steps:
a)
Replace the nose gear steering system cable loop. These are the tasks:
  • Nose Gear Steering System Cables Removal, AMM TASK 32-51-31-000-802,
  • Nose Gear Steering System Cables Installation, AMM TASK 32-51-31-400-802.
b)
Do the "Test of the Normal Steering Response" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
c)
If the nose wheel steering system operates correctly, then you corrected the fault.
‹1›
Do the "Put the Airplane Back to its Usual Condition" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
2)
For all other problems found during the inspection, do these steps:
a)
Repair or replace the bad components.
b)
Do this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Adjustment, AMM TASK 32-51-00-820-805.
c)
Do the "Test of the Normal Steering Response" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
d)
If the nose wheel steering system operates correctly, then you corrected the fault.
‹1›
Do the "Put the Airplane Back to its Usual Condition" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
(c)
If you do not find a problem during the inspection, then continue.
(2)
Do this check of the movement of the nose landing gear steering summing mechanism.
(a)
Move the towing lever to the TOW position.
(b)
Install the towing lever lockpin.
(c)
Remove the nose landing gear steering summing mechanism cover and disconnect the link assembly from the steering metering valve. The necessary steps are in this task:
  • Steering Metering Valve Removal, AMM TASK 32-51-11-000-801.
(d)
Turn the nose landing gear steering control wheel (tiller) clockwise to the stop, and then turn it counterclockwise to the stop.
(e)
If the nose landing gear steering summing mechanism does not move freely when you turn the nose landing gear steering control wheel (tiller), then do these steps:
1)
Repair or replace the pulleys in the nose wheel steering cable loop.
2)
Do this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Adjustment, AMM TASK 32-51-00-820-805.
3)
Do the "Test of the Normal Steering Response" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
4)
If the nose wheel steering system operates correctly, then you corrected the fault.
a)
Do the "Put the Airplane Back to its Usual Condition" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
(f)
If the nose landing gear steering summing mechanism does move freely with light tension in the centering spring, then continue.
(3)
Do a check of the torque of the nose landing gear steering control wheel (tiller) nut that holds the control wheel to the tiller assembly. The applicable steps are in this task:
  • Nose Landing Gear Steering Control Wheel Installation, AMM TASK 32-51-41-400-801.
(a)
If the torque is not as specified in the AMM task, then do these steps:
1)
Remove and re-install the nose landing gear steering control wheel (tiller). These are the tasks:
  • Nose Landing Gear Steering Control Wheel Removal, AMM TASK 32-51-41-000-801,
  • Nose Landing Gear Steering Control Wheel Installation, AMM TASK 32-51-41-400-801.
2)
Do the "Test of the Normal Steering Response" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
3)
If the nose wheel steering system operates correctly, then you corrected the fault.
a)
Do the "Put the Airplane Back to its Usual Condition" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
(b)
If the torque is as specified in the AMM task, then continue.
(4)
Do this check of the steering metering valve and the left and right nose gear steering actuators:
(a)
Remove the towing lever lockpin and let the towing lever move to the NORMAL POSITION.
(b)
Turn the captain's nose landing gear steering control wheel (tiller) clockwise to the stop, and then turn it counterclockwise to the stop several times.
(c)
Look and listen for hydraulic leakage.
(d)
Feel for heat at the steering metering valve and the left and right nose gear steering actuators.
(e)
If you find signs of hydraulic leakage at the steering metering valve, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the leaks or replace the steering metering valve. To replace the steering metering valve, do these tasks:
  • Steering Metering Valve Removal, AMM TASK 32-51-11-000-801,
  • Steering Metering Valve Installation, AMM TASK 32-51-11-400-801.
2)
Do the "Test of the Normal Steering Response" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
3)
If the nose wheel steering system operates correctly, then you corrected the fault.
a)
Do the "Put the Airplane Back to its Usual Condition" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
(f)
If you find signs of hydraulic leakage at the left and right nose gear steering actuators, then do these steps:
1)
Repair the leaks or replace the left and right nose gear steering actuators. To replace the left and right nose gear steering actuators, do these tasks:
  • Nose Gear Steering Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 32-51-51-000-801,
  • Nose Gear Steering Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 32-51-51-400-801.
2)
Do the "Test of the Normal Steering Response" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
3)
If the nose wheel steering system operates correctly, then you corrected the fault.
a)
Do the "Put the Airplane Back to its Usual Condition" part of this task:
  • Nose Wheel Steering System Test (Airplane on Ground), AMM TASK 32-51-00-700-801.
(g)
If you do not find any signs of hydraulic leakage, then continue.
1)
Move the towing lever to the TOW POSITION.
2)
Re-install the towing lever lockpin.
