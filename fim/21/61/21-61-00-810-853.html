Cannot Cool Cabins With All Temperature Selectors In Full COLD - Fault Isolation
A.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
Do this task:
  • Air Conditioning, System Test, Air Conditioning IBIT, AMM TASK 21-00-00-730-801.
(2)
If you find any maintenance messages that show on the ground test display, then do these steps:
(a)
Do the fault isolation task for each maintenance message before you continue.
(3)
Do this task to examine the performance of the air conditioning system:
  • Ground Pack Run Test - APU AUTO Flow Mode, AMM TASK 21-51-00-700-804.
(a)
The correct temperature for the LEFT/RIGHT PACK SENSOR TEMP is 86 degrees F (30 degrees C) or less at 15 minutes.
(b)
The correct temperature for the CONT CAB ZONE TEMP is 50 degrees F (10 degrees C) or less at 15 minutes.
(c)
The correct temperature for the FORWARD/AFT ZONE DUCT TEMP is 68 degrees F (20 degrees C) or less at 15 minutes.
(d)
Look for signs of leakage from the duct joints.
(e)
If one or more of the temperatures is not correct, then do these steps:
1)
If the LEFT PACK SENSOR TEMP or the RIGHT PACK SENSOR TEMP is not correct, then do these steps:
a)
If you find leakage, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Leakage Checks) below.
b)
If you find the left STCV or the right STCV is OPEN, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (STCV Open) below.
c)
If you find that the left STCV and the right STCV is CLOSED, and that the left TCV and the right TCV is CLOSED, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (STCV Closed) below.
2)
If the LEFT PACK SENSOR TEMP and the RIGHT PACK SENSOR TEMP is correct, then do these steps:
a)
If the CONT CAB ZONE DUCT TEMP, or the FORWARD ZONE DUCT TEMP, or the AFT ZONE DUCT TEMP is not correct, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Temperature Control and Air Distribution) below.
b)
If you find that there is ice on the left conditioned air check valve, and the right conditioned air check valve, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Temperature Control and Air Distribution) below.
(f)
If all the temperatures are correct, then there was an intermittent fault.
