Trailing Edge Flaps Operate Too Slowly - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
The trailing edge flaps use hydraulic power for primary operation and electrical power for alternate operation.
(2)
Hydraulic power to the flap hydraulic motor is controlled by the flap control valve, flap priority valve and the flap bypass valve.
B.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
Make sure that you use a hydraulic cart or the engine-driven pump to supply system B hydraulic power.
NOTE:  The trailing edge flap system needs approximately 14 gallons per minute to operate at the normal speed. The electric motor-driven pump will not supply sufficient hydraulic power for the test.
(2)
Measure the time necessary to extend and retract the trailing edge flaps with primary control. The applicable steps are in this task:
  • Trailing Edge Flap Bypass Valve Functional Test, AMM TASK 27-51-02-720-801.
(a)
Measure the time necessary for the trailing edge flaps to extend from the UP position to the 1-unit position.
(b)
Measure the time necessary for the trailing edge flaps to retract from the 1-unit position to the UP position.
(3)
Measure the time necessary to extend and retract the trailing edge flaps with alternate control. The applicable steps are in this task:
  • Alternate Flap Control System Functional Test, AMM TASK 27-51-00-720-803.
(a)
Measure the time necessary for the trailing edge flaps to extend from the UP position to the 1-unit position.
(b)
Measure the time necessary for the trailing edge flaps to retract from the 1-unit position to the UP position.
(4)
If the time necessary to extend and retract the trailing edge flaps between the UP position and the 1-unit position was more than the specified limits with primary and alternate control, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Flap Drive System) below.
(5)
If the time necessary to extend and retract the trailing edge flaps between the UP position and the 1-unit position was more than the specified limits with only primary control, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Hydraulic) below.
(6)
If the time necessary to extend and retract the trailing edge flaps between the UP position and the 1-unit position was more than the specified limits with only alternate control, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Electric) below.
(7)
If the time necessary to extend and retract the trailing edge flaps between the UP position and the 1-unit position was within the specified limits with primary and alternate control, then there was an intermittent fault.
C.
Fault Isolation Procedure (Flap Drive System)
(1)
Do a detailed visual inspection of the left and right wing TE flap drive system and its torque tubes, couplings, gearboxes, linkages, and actuators. This is the task:
  • Trailing Edge Flap Drive System Inspection, AMM TASK 27-51-00-210-801.
(a)
Look for missing or loose parts, interference, wear, or damage.
(b)
If you find a problem during the inspection, then do these steps:
1)
Repair or replace the bad components.
2)
Measure the time necessary to extend and retract the trailing edge flaps between the UP position and the 1-unit position with primary control. The applicable steps are in this task:
  • Trailing Edge Flap Bypass Valve Functional Test, AMM TASK 27-51-02-720-801.
