Leading Edge Flaps and Slats Do Not Retract (LE Lockout) - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
Leading edge lockouts are usually caused by excessive internal leakage in the leading edge slat actuators that set the hydraulic fuses.
B.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
Make sure that the leading edge system is locked out in the mid-extend position.
(a)
If the leading edge system is not in the lockout condition, then do these steps to simulate the lockout condition:
1)
Supply system B hydraulic power. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Pressurization, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-801.
2)
Move the flap control lever to the 1-unit position to extend the leading edge flaps and slats.
3)
Wait 20 to 30 minutes after the leading edge flaps and slats stop to move.
4)
Move the flap control lever to the UP position to retract the leading edge flaps and slats.
5)
If the leading edge flaps and slats retract, then do these steps:
NOTE:  If the flaps and slats retracted, then you did not simulate the lockout condition.
a)
Move the flap control lever to the 1-unit position to extend the leading edge flaps and slats.
b)
Wait 60 minutes after the leading edge flaps and slats stop to move.
c)
Move the flap control lever to the UP position to retract the leading edge flaps and slats.
d)
If the leading edge flaps and slats retract, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Hydraulic Problem) below.
NOTE:  If the flaps and slats retracted, then you cannot simulate the lockout condition.
e)
If the leading edge flaps and slats do not retract, then continue.
NOTE:  If the flaps and slats did not retract, then you simulated the lockout condition.
(b)
If the leading edge system is in the lockout condition, then continue.
(2)
Monitor the movement of the leading edge flaps and slats while you do the next step.
(3)
Open this circuit breaker: C210, FLIGHT CONTROL FLAP SHUTOFF VALVES [P6-2, A7].
(4)
If the leading edge flaps and slats do not retract, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Hydraulic Problem) below.
(a)
Close this circuit breaker: C210, FLIGHT CONTROL FLAP SHUTOFF VALVES [P6-2, A7].
(5)
If the leading edge flaps and slats retract, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Electrical Problem) below.
(a)
Close this circuit breaker: C210, FLIGHT CONTROL FLAP SHUTOFF VALVES [P6-2, A7].
C.
Fault Isolation Procedure (Hydraulic Problem)
(1)
Reset the system B hydraulic fuses. This is the task:
  • Flap and Slat Hydraulic Fuses (System B) Reset, AMM TASK 27-81-61-860-801.
(2)
Do these steps to check for actuator internal leakage without breaking into the hydraulic system:
NOTE:  The following steps require the use of the stethoscope, COM-1797 or the Infrared Thermometer, COM-1572. If you do not have these tools, then go to the next step.
 MAKE SURE ALL PERSONS AND EQUIPMENT ARE CLEAR OF THE FLIGHT CONTROLS SURFACES, THE THRUST REVERSERS, AND THE LANDING GEAR. THESE COMPONENTS CAN MOVE SUDDENLY WHEN YOU SUPPLY HYDRAULIC POWER. THIS CAN CAUSE INJURY TO PERSONS AND DAMAGE TO EQUIPMENT.
(a)
Supply system B hydraulic power. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Pressurization, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-801.
(b)
Move the flap control lever to the 1-unit position to extend the leading edge flaps and slats to the mid-extend position.
(c)
If the Infrared Thermometer, COM-1572 is available, then use it to measure and record the temperatures of all 8 LE slat actuators and all 4 LE flap actuators.
NOTE:  The larger part of the actuator barrel is a good location to measure the temperatures of each actuator.
1)
If the temperature of one or more LE flap or slat actuators is 10 degrees higher than the others, then do these steps:
a)
Replace the applicable leading edge flap actuator. These are the tasks:
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-31-000-801,
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-31-400-801.
b)
Replace the applicable leading edge slat actuator. These are the tasks:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-41-000-801,
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-41-400-801.
c)
If the fault does not occur on the subsequent flights, then you corrected the fault.
2)
If the temperature of all LE flap and slat actuators are within 10 degrees of each other, then continue.
(d)
If the stethoscope, COM-1797 is available, then use it to listen to the internal leakage of all 8 LE slat and all 4 LE flap actuators.
NOTE:  The actuator manifold next to the "P" valve port is a good location to put the stethoscope and listen for internal leakage of each actuator.
1)
If you are sure that the leaking noise from one or more LE flap or slat actuators is significantly more than the others, then do these steps:
a)
Replace the applicable leading edge flap actuator. These are the tasks:
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-31-000-801,
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-31-400-801.
b)
Replace the applicable leading edge slat actuator. These are the tasks:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-41-000-801,
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-41-400-801.
c)
If the fault does not occur on the subsequent flights, then you corrected the fault.
2)
If you are not sure that there is significant leaking noise from one or more flap or slat actuators, then continue.
(3)
Measure the internal leakage in the leading edge system. This is the task:
  • Leading Edge Flap and Slat System Internal Hydraulic Leakage Check, AMM TASK 27-81-00-790-801.
(a)
If the total leakage rate is more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then do these steps:
1)
Measure the internal leakage across the autoslat control valve, V132. This is the task:
  • Autoslat Valve Internal Hydraulic Leakage Check, AMM TASK 27-83-21-790-801.
a)
If the leakage rate is more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then do these steps:
‹1›
Replace the autoslat control valve, V132. These are the tasks:
  • Autoslat Control Valve Removal, AMM TASK 27-83-21-000-801,
  • Autoslat Control Valve Installation, AMM TASK 27-83-21-400-801.
‹2›
If the fault does not occur on the subsequent flights, then you corrected the fault.
b)
If the leakage rate is not more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then continue.
2)
Measure the internal leakage across the LE uncommanded motion shutoff valve, V162. This is the task:
  • Leading Edge Flap and Slat System Internal Hydraulic Leakage Check, AMM TASK 27-81-00-790-801.
a)
If the leakage rate is more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then do these steps:
‹1›
Replace the LE uncommanded motion shutoff valve, V162. These are the tasks:
  • LE Uncommanded Motion (UCM) or LE Cruise Depressurization Valve Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-52-000-801,
  • LE Uncommanded Motion (UCM) or LE Cruise Depressurization Valve Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-52-400-801.
‹2›
If the fault does not occur on the subsequent flights, then you corrected the fault.
b)
If the leakage rate is not more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then continue.
3)
Measure the internal leakage across the LE cruise depressurization valve, V163. This is the task:
  • Leading Edge Cruise Depressurization Valve Hydraulic Internal Leakage Check, AMM TASK 27-81-52-790-801.
a)
If the leakage rate is more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then do these steps:
‹1›
Replace the LE cruise depressurization valve, V163. These are the tasks:
  • LE Uncommanded Motion (UCM) or LE Cruise Depressurization Valve Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-52-000-801,
  • LE Uncommanded Motion (UCM) or LE Cruise Depressurization Valve Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-52-400-801.
‹2›
If the fault does not occur on the subsequent flights, then you corrected the fault.
b)
If the leakage rate is not more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then continue.
4)
Measure the internal leakage across the leading edge flap and slat control valve. This is the task:
  • Leading Edge Flap and Slat System Internal Hydraulic Leakage Check, AMM TASK 27-81-00-790-801.
a)
If the leakage rate is more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then do these steps:
‹1›
Replace the leading edge flap and slat control valve. These are the tasks:
  • Leading Edge Flap and Slat Control Valve Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-81-000-801,
  • Leading Edge Flap and Slat Control Valve Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-81-400-801.
‹2›
If the fault does not occur on the subsequent flights, then you corrected the fault.
b)
If the leakage rate is not more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then continue.
(b)
If the total leakage rate is not more than the rate specified in the AMM task, then continue.
(4)
Do these steps to measure the internal leakage across each LE flap and slat actuator.
 MAKE SURE ALL PERSONS AND EQUIPMENT ARE CLEAR OF THE FLIGHT CONTROLS SURFACES, THE THRUST REVERSERS, AND THE LANDING GEAR. THESE COMPONENTS CAN MOVE SUDDENLY WHEN YOU SUPPLY HYDRAULIC POWER. THIS CAN CAUSE INJURY TO PERSONS AND DAMAGE TO EQUIPMENT.
(a)
Supply system B hydraulic power. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Pressurization, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-801.
(b)
Move the flap control lever to the 1-unit position to extend the leading edge flaps and slats to the mid-extend position.
(c)
Remove the system B hydraulic power. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Power Removal, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-805.
(d)
Remove the pressure from the system B reservoir. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic Reservoirs Depressurization, AMM TASK 29-09-00-860-802.
 DO NOT MOVE THE LEADING EDGE DEVICES WHILE YOU DO THIS ACTUATOR LEAKAGE CHECK. IF THE LEADING EDGE DEVICES MOVE WITH THE HOSES CONNECTED, THEN THE HOSES CAN DAMAGE THE LEADING EDGE STRUCTURE.
(e)
Do these steps to disconnect each actuator's hydraulic lines and collect the fluid at these two locations:
1)
The actuator's Standby line:
a)
Disconnect the line at any point between the actuator and the "T".
‹1›
To disconnect the lines from the LE flap actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-31-000-801.
‹2›
To disconnect the lines from the LE slat actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-41-000-801.
b)
Put a cap or plug on the port that is closest to the "T".
c)
Connect a hose to the port closest to the actuator and collect the fluid from the hose in the graduated (scaled) collector, STD-1223.
2)
The actuator's C2 line:
NOTE:  This step only applies to the LE slat actuators. The LE flap actuators do not have a C2 line.
a)
Disconnect the line at any point between the actuator and the "T".
‹1›
To disconnect the lines from the LE slat actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-41-000-801.
b)
Put a cap or plug on the port that is closest to the "T".
c)
Connect a hose to the port closest to the actuator and collect the fluid from the hose in the graduated (scaled) collector, STD-1223.
 MAKE SURE ALL PERSONS AND EQUIPMENT ARE CLEAR OF THE FLIGHT CONTROLS SURFACES, THE THRUST REVERSERS, AND THE LANDING GEAR. THESE COMPONENTS CAN MOVE SUDDENLY WHEN YOU SUPPLY HYDRAULIC POWER. THIS CAN CAUSE INJURY TO PERSONS AND DAMAGE TO EQUIPMENT.
(f)
Supply system B hydraulic power. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Pressurization, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-801.
(g)
Measure and record the leakage rate from both of the hoses for each leading edge flap and slat actuator for a minimum of 1 minute.
1)
If the combined leakage rate from both of the hoses for each leading edge flap and slat actuator is not more than 15 cc/minute, no maintenance action is necessary.
a)
Remove the hoses and re-connect the hydraulic lines that you disconnected.
‹1›
To re-connect the lines for the LE flap actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-31-400-801.
‹2›
To re-connect the lines for the LE slat actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-41-400-801.
2)
If the combined leakage rate from both of the hoses for one or more of the leading edge flap or slat actuators is more than 15 cc/minute, then do these steps before you continue:
a)
Replace the applicable leading edge flap actuator. These are the tasks:
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-31-000-801,
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-31-400-801.
b)
Replace the applicable leading edge slat actuator. These are the tasks:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-41-000-801,
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-41-400-801.
c)
Remove the hoses and re-connect the hydraulic lines that you disconnected.
‹1›
To re-connect the lines for the LE flap actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-31-400-801.
‹2›
To re-connect the lines for the LE slat actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-41-400-801.
(5)
Do these steps to flush the leading edge system:
NOTE:  Start the flush from inboard to outboard.
 MAKE SURE ALL PERSONS AND EQUIPMENT ARE CLEAR OF THE FLIGHT CONTROLS SURFACES, THE THRUST REVERSERS, AND THE LANDING GEAR. THESE COMPONENTS CAN MOVE SUDDENLY WHEN YOU SUPPLY HYDRAULIC POWER. THIS CAN CAUSE INJURY TO PERSONS AND DAMAGE TO EQUIPMENT.
(a)
Supply system B hydraulic power. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Pressurization, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-801.
(b)
Move the flap control lever to the 30-unit position to extend the leading edge flaps and slats to the full-extend position.
(c)
Remove the system B hydraulic power. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Power Removal, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-805.
(d)
Remove the pressure from the system B reservoir. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic Reservoirs Depressurization, AMM TASK 29-09-00-860-802.
 DO NOT MOVE THE LEADING EDGE DEVICES WHILE YOU DO THIS ACTUATOR LEAKAGE CHECK. IF THE LEADING EDGE DEVICES MOVE WITH THE HOSES CONNECTED, THEN THE HOSES CAN DAMAGE THE LEADING EDGE STRUCTURE.
(e)
Do these steps for each leading edge flap and slat actuator:
1)
Disconnect each of these leading edge flap and slat actuator's hydraulic lines:
a)
The actuator's retract pressure line:
‹1›
Disconnect the actuator's retract pressure line at the swivel.
‹a›
To disconnect the lines from the LE flap actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-31-000-801.
‹b›
To disconnect the lines from the LE slat actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-41-000-801.
‹2›
Connect a hose to the port closest to the "T" and collect the fluid from the hose in the graduated (scaled) collector, STD-1223.
NOTE:  Try to prevent hydraulic fluid splatter.
‹3›
Leave the port that is closest to the actuator open.
b)
The actuator's C1 line:
‹1›
Disconnect the actuator's C1 line at the swivel.
‹a›
To disconnect the lines from the LE flap actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-31-000-801.
‹b›
To disconnect the lines from the LE slat actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-41-000-801.
‹2›
Connect a hose to the port closest to the "T" and collect the fluid from the hose in the graduated (scaled) collector, STD-1223.
NOTE:  Try to prevent hydraulic fluid splatter.
‹3›
Leave the port that is closest to the actuator open.
c)
The actuator's C2 line:
NOTE:  This step only applies to the LE slat actuators. The LE flap actuators do not have a C2 line.
‹1›
Disconnect the actuator's C2 line at the swivel.
‹a›
To disconnect the lines from the LE slat actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Removal, AMM TASK 27-81-41-000-801.
‹b›
Connect a hose to the port closest to the "T" and collect the fluid from the hose in the graduated (scaled) collector, STD-1223.
NOTE:  Try to prevent hydraulic fluid splatter.
‹c›
Leave the port that is closest to the actuator open.
 MAKE SURE ALL PERSONS AND EQUIPMENT ARE CLEAR OF THE FLIGHT CONTROLS SURFACES, THE THRUST REVERSERS, AND THE LANDING GEAR. THESE COMPONENTS CAN MOVE SUDDENLY WHEN YOU SUPPLY HYDRAULIC POWER. THIS CAN CAUSE INJURY TO PERSONS AND DAMAGE TO EQUIPMENT.
2)
Use the EMDP to pressurize hydraulic system B. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Pressurization, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-801.
a)
Use the HYD PUMPS B ELEC 1 switch to operate the EMDP for 2 seconds and then turn it off.
NOTE:  The pump should be on long enough to flush out half a gallon of fluid. If the pump is on too long, then the LE system's fuses can set.
3)
Remove the hoses and re-connect the hydraulic lines that you disconnected.
a)
To re-connect the lines for the LE flap actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Flap Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-31-400-801.
b)
To re-connect the lines for the LE slat actuator, do the applicable steps in this task:
  • Leading Edge Slat Actuator Installation, AMM TASK 27-81-41-400-801.
4)
Add fluid to the B hydraulic system's reservoir if necessary. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic Reservoir Servicing, AMM TASK 12-12-00-610-801.
5)
If the fault does not occur on the subsequent flights, then you corrected the fault.
D.
Fault Isolation Procedure (Electrical Problem)
(1)
Go to the FIM task for the observed fault "Leading edge flaps and slats: do not operate with primary control" and do the steps in the task.
(2)
If the fault does not occur on the subsequent flights, then you corrected the fault.
