Leading Edge Flaps and Slats Do Not Operate (With Primary Control) - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
The leading edge flaps and slats do not extend and retract with flap control lever at each position.
(2)
The primary control system uses the hydraulic system B to move the flaps and slats.
(3)
Hydraulic pressure goes through the LE cruise depressurization valve to the LE flap and slat control valve. The LE flap and slat control valve sends extend pressure to the LE flap and slat actuators. This causes the LE flaps and slats to move to the extend position. The hydraulic fluid goes through hydraulic fuses in the lines.
B.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
Make sure that if the system B hydraulic power is on, it stays on.
NOTE:  The hydraulic fuses will reset if you remove hydraulic power. This can make it more difficult to find the problem.
(2)
Do a visual check of the position of the leading edge flaps and slats.
(a)
If the position of one surface is very different from the positions of the other surfaces, then make a note of it and then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Align) below.
NOTE:  In-flight airload can cause misalignment between the flaps and slats.
(b)
If the positions of all the surfaces align, then continue.
(3)
Do a check of your maintenance records for history that the leading edge flaps and slats did not retract from the mid-extend position.
(a)
If you find history of this fault, then make a note of it and do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Related Task) below.
(b)
If you do not find history of this fault, then continue.
(4)
Do this task:
  • FSEU Display and Existing Faults Test, AMM TASK 27-51-00-740-801.
(a)
Look in the FSEU display or OMF Existing Faults for these related maintenance messages: 27‑81352, 27‑81353, 27‑81354, 27‑81355, 27‑81357, 27‑81358, 27‑81359, or 27‑81360.
(b)
If you find any of the related maintenance messages, then do the FIM task for each related maintenance message before you continue.
(5)
Do these steps to extend and retract the trailing edge flaps and leading edge flaps and slats:
(a)
Supply system B hydraulic power if it is necessary. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Pressurization, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-801.
(b)
Move the flap control lever to extend and retract the trailing edge flaps and leading edge flaps and slats. The applicable steps are in this task:
  • Leading Edge Flap and Slat System Operation With Primary Control, AMM TASK 27-81-00-860-801.
