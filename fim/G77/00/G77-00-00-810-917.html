Maintenance Message 77-44610 - Fault Isolation
ENGINE N1 VIBRATION IS APPROACHING EXCEEDANCE LIMIT
Detected by: ENGINE-1 EEC
A.
Description
(1)
The valid selected N1 vibration value is greater than or equal to N1 approaching exceedance limit. N2 is at or above idle.
(2)
Vibration sensor faults are inhibited below 34% N1 for fan/LPT vibration signals and 60% N2 for core vibration signals
(3)
While you operate the engine during the survey, monitor the engine vibration signals on EPCS page 3.
(a)
Rapid fluctuation in indicated vibration that occurs without changes in engine parameters or airframe vibration is an indication of vibration sensor issue.
1)
Parameters on EPCS page 3 can help isolate erroneous indication to a specific sensor or signal.
B.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
Do a visual check of the turbine exhaust area in engine 1 for broken blades, metal particles, or signs of internal damage.
(2)
If you see signs of damage, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Damaged Engine) below.
(3)
If flight crew reports show high vibration during engine start, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Vibration During Start) below.
(4)
If flight crew reports show high vibration after engine start, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Vibration During Operation) below.
C.
Fault Isolation Procedure (Damaged Engine)
(1)
Replace the engine 1 assembly, 00002. These are the tasks:
  • Power Plant - Removal, AMM TASK G71-00-02-000-801-G00,
  • Power Plant - Installation, AMM TASK G71-00-02-400-801-G00.
(a)
For the replaced engine, do the required power plant test in the table of this task:
  • Power Plant Test Reference Table, AMM TASK G71-00-00-800-804-G00.
(b)
If the engine operates correctly, then you corrected the fault.
