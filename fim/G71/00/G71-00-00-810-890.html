Surge or Stall Of Engine During Forward Thrust (Engine 1) - Fault Isolation
A.
Description
(1)
You saw or heard indications of a surge or a stall.
(2)
The engine exhaust gas temperature (EGT) indication shows that the EGT is higher than the EGT redline limit.
B.
Fault Isolation Procedure
(1)
For engine 1, download the EEC data and send it to General Electric/CFM. This is the task:
  • Electronic Engine Control (EEC) Data Download (On-Wing)(Selection), AMM TASK G73-21-05-970-803-G00.
(2)
Download the DFDAU and QAR data and send it to Boeing. This is the task:
  • Data Download From Flight Data Recorder System (Selection), AMM TASK 31-31-00-970-803.
(3)
Look for flight crew reports of these conditions:
(a)
Flight Crew Reports
1)
Operation in ice crystal icing conditions
2)
Signs of ice ingestion
3)
Uncommanded N1 reduction
4)
Engine stall
5)
Engine surge
6)
Engine flameout
7)
Engine thrust loss
8)
Sub-idle N2.
(b)
If you found an applicable flight crew report, then go to the FIM task for "Engine: unusual operation during ice crystal icing conditions" and do the steps in the task.
(4)
If the surge or stall occurred on the ground during crosswinds or tailwinds, then do these steps:
(a)
Move the airplane to operate the engine into the wind.
(b)
Operate the engine at the thrust levels and ECS service loads that the engine had when the surge occurred.
(c)
If the engine operates normally, then return the engine to service. Monitor the engine operation on the subsequent flights.
(5)
Look for flight crew reports of an overtemperature event during a surge or stall of engine 1.
(a)
If you find an overtemperature event, then do these steps:
1)
Do this task:
  • Overtemperature Inspection, AMM TASK G71-00-00-200-810-G00.
2)
Do the corrective action specified in the AMM task.
3)
For the component in the previous step, make a list of the required power plant tests from the table in this task:
  • Power Plant Test Reference Table, AMM TASK G71-00-00-800-804-G00.
4)
Find the tests in your list that do not require engine operation. Do these tests first.
5)
Find the tests in your list that require engine operation. Do these tests after you complete the tests that do not require engine operation.
NOTE:  You can operate the engine one time for multiple tests.
6)
For engine 1, do this task:
  • Test No. 6 - Power Assurance Test, AMM TASK G71-00-00-720-802-G00.
7)
If the tests were successful and there were no maintenance messages, then you corrected the fault.
(b)
If you do not find an overtemperature event, then continue.
