ELEC 2 LOW PRESSURE Light Is On - Fault Isolation
OMR 001-018
A.
Description
(1)
The ELEC 2 LOW PRESSURE light comes on when the electric motor-driven pump (EMDP) for the hydraulic system A is below 1200 psi.
(2)
This is normal operation of the ELEC 2 LOW PRESSURE light:
(a)
When the system A EMDP operates, the ELEC 2 LOW PRESSURE light is off.
(b)
When the pressure of the system A EMDP is below normal, the ELEC 2 LOW PRESSURE light will come on.
(c)
When the system A EMDP does not operate, the ELEC 2 LOW PRESSURE light will come on.
OMR ALL
B.
Initial Evaluation
(1)
Make sure that the FLT CONTROL and SPOILER switches on the overhead panel are OFF.
 MAKE SURE ALL PERSONS AND EQUIPMENT ARE CLEAR OF THE FLIGHT CONTROLS SURFACES, THE THRUST REVERSERS, AND THE LANDING GEAR. THESE COMPONENTS CAN MOVE SUDDENLY WHEN YOU SUPPLY HYDRAULIC POWER. THIS CAN CAUSE INJURY TO PERSONS AND DAMAGE TO EQUIPMENT.
(2)
Use the EMDP to supply pressure to the A hydraulic system. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System Pressurization with an Electric Motor-Driven Pump (EMDP), AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-803.
(3)
Push the SYS button on the multi-function panel and find the HYDRAULIC PRESS A indication on the display.
(4)
Make a note of the HYDRAULIC PRESS A indication.
(5)
If the system A EMDP does not operate, or the pressure is not stable, then do these steps:
(a)
Release the pressure in the hydraulic system. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Power Removal, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-805.
(b)
Do the Fault Isolation Procedure (EMDP Operation) below.
(6)
If the indication is less than 2800, then do these steps:
(a)
Release the pressure in the hydraulic system. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Power Removal, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-805.
(b)
Do the Fault Isolation Procedure (EMDP Pressure) below.
(7)
If the indication is more than 2800, then do these steps:
(a)
Set the FLT CONTROL and SPOILER switches to ON.
(b)
Make a note of the HYDRAULIC PRESS A indication.
(c)
Release the pressure in the hydraulic system. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Power Removal, AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-805.
(d)
Set the FLT CONTROL and SPOILER switches to OFF.
(e)
If the indication was less than 2800 with the switches at ON, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (Internal Leakage) below.
(f)
If the indication was more than 2800 with the switches at ON, then do these steps:
1)
Use the EDP to supply pressure to the A hydraulic system. This is the task:
  • Hydraulic System A or B Pressurization with an Engine-Driven Pump (EDP), AMM TASK 29-11-00-860-804.
(g)
Make a note of the HYDRAULIC PRESS A indication.
(h)
Release the pressure in the hydraulic system.
(i)
If the indication was less than 2800, then do the Fault Isolation Procedure (EDP) below.
(j)
If the indication was more than 2800, then there was an intermittent fault.
